# ADR-008: Combined risk, ranked actions and trade ideas

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-05

## Context

Slide 07 of the wireframes combines the rubric with market-signal impact into a decision matrix, a
composite gauge and a ranked action list with suitability checks and approve-and-log. The brief adds
trade ideas driven by signals, the current portfolio and the rubric. The RM must remain the decision
maker and every recommendation must show its evidence.

## Decision

### Combined risk

- **Vulnerability** (rows) from the rubric and its mismatches: Capacity 1 or a critical mismatch is
  high; Capacity 2, Horizon 1 or a warning mismatch is medium; margin-call proximity and liquidity
  shortfall alerts raise it independently. Without a rubric, alerts alone decide and the page says so.
- **Signal severity** (columns) from the impact engine: the up-to-five most exposed signals of the
  last 90 dataset days reaching the household are run at base and severe severity; base-case loss
  worse than 5% is high, worse than 2% medium. High or severe signals reaching 20% of the household
  lift the level one step. If the impact engine is unavailable the level falls back to exposure.
- **Matrix cell** is the wireframe grid (Monitor / Review / Discuss / Act Now / URGENT) and sets the
  default urgency of generated actions.
- **Composite gauge** 0–10 = signal severity (severe-case loss, 10% maps to 4) + rubric mismatch
  (1.5 per critical, 0.75 per warning, +1 for Capacity 1, capped 3.5) + concentration (largest
  look-through name against its limit, mandate drift, capped 2.5). The decomposition and formula
  are shown beside the gauge.

### Ranked actions

Generated by rules from alerts (collateral, mandate, concentration, look-through, exclusion, KYC,
cash need, liquidity, unanswered contact, stale valuation), from the most exposed recent signal (a
client call) and from the rubric (re-confirm the profile when Appetite is 1 and equity is over its
band). Each action carries evidence, benefit, trade-off, sources (alert and signal ids), a
suitability result with named checks, and a score of urgency × 2 + severity + deadline proximity +
suitability that sets the rank. Ids are deterministic hashes of client, category and title so RM
decisions survive regeneration.

### Trade ideas

Rules over mandate status, look-through exposure, liquidity coverage, the rubric and recent rate
signals: trim an overweight class to its ceiling and fund the most underweight class; trim a single
line to its limit into a diversified fund of the same class; replace excluded holdings in
exclusion-bound mandates; reduce look-through concentration where only the direct line is saleable;
raise cash to 1.5× coverage; shorten duration when rates rose and the horizon is short; deploy cash
above the band toward target. Instruments come from the loaded universe by attribute, never by name.
Every idea passes a suitability gate: human approval, Appetite and Horizon consistency (blocking),
mandate direction, single-position limit, exclusions, liquidity of the sale, waivers and client
statements found in the notes (review). Blocked ideas remain visible with the failing rule.
Confidence starts at 0.75–0.9 by rule and is reduced for heuristics, stale marks and a missing rubric.

### Decisions and audit

Approve or reject writes `derived.action_decisions` and an audit event with the RM id from the
dataset context. The summary states that no order was placed. Nothing in the workbench executes a
trade.

## Consequences

- For Lau Chi Ming the matrix reads High vulnerability × Medium signal = Act Now, the first action
  is the collateral conversation, and the direct-property trim is blocked because the asset is
  illiquid, which the RM sees rather than infers.
- Severity of signal risk depends on the impact engine's simple models and additive stacking;
  the "Why" drawer states both.
- Because generation is deterministic and decisions are stored by id, the queue can be regenerated
  at any clock while the RM's decisions persist.
